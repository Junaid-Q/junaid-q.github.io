Research and Development Projects

5G INDUCE Project (EU Horizon 2020)

Focused on developing a secure 5G edge architecture for industrial IoT systems, integrating virtualized network functions and low-latency communication.

IMU-based Center of Pressure Prediction

Designed and trained deep learning models (LSTM, GRU, CNN-BiLSTM, and TCN-Attention) for predicting postural stability using wearable IMU data.

Lightweight Key Management for LoRaWAN

Proposed an AES-128 and ECDH-based cryptographic scheme validated with Scyther to enhance IoT communication security.

Smart Grid Security and Privacy

Contributed to a book chapter addressing confidentiality, integrity, and device lifecycle protection mechanisms for modern power systems.

Underwater Wireless Sensor Networks (UWSNs)

Developed routing protocols for delay minimization and energy-efficient data transmission in dense UWSNs.
