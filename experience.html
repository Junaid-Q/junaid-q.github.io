Postdoctoral Research Experience

Postdoctoral Researcher – Department of Computer Science, School of Science and Technology, Örebro University, Sweden (April 2026–Present)

  • Supported by the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program (WASP) and affiliated with the AI, Robotics and Cybersecurity Center (ARC).
  • Conducting research on cybersecurity for embodied AI systems and autonomous sensor-driven platforms.
  • Working on threat modeling, intrusion detection, and resilience mechanisms for AI-integrated cyber-physical systems.

Postdoctoral Researcher – DSP Laboratory, DITEN, University of Genoa, Italy (2024–2025)

  • Conducted research on human postural stability prediction using IMU-equipped glasses under the RAISE (PNRR NextGenerationEU) project.
  • Developed hybrid deep learning architectures (LSTM, GRU, TCN-Attention, CNN–BiLSTM–Attention) for Center of Pressure (CoP) trajectory estimation.
  • Applied Explainable AI (XAI) methods including Integrated Gradients, SHAP, Attention mechanisms, and Fidelity Masking for interpretability.
  • Collaborated with biomedical engineers and clinicians to enhance model transparency and physiological relevance.

Postdoctoral Researcher – CNIT (National Inter-University Consortium for Telecommunications), Genoa, Italy (2023–2024)

  • Contributed to the Horizon Europe 6Green and 5G-INDUCE (H2020) projects on secure 5G edge-cloud architectures.
  • Developed containerized VNFs and orchestration pipelines for industrial IoT scenarios using OpenStack and Docker.
  • Proposed lightweight cryptographic mechanisms for device authentication and edge data protection.
